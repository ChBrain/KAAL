# Requirement: a-drawing-fixes-more-than-structure

_Written in analyse mode, in an analyst run over twenty unconsumed retros,
ten of the architect skill and ten of the code skill. This is the first of
three tasks from that run. Four of the ten architect retros record "Lacked:
nothing new"; the items behind this task are the twenty-fifth's and the
twenty-eighth's._

## Goal

Whoever draws a change that is text rather than structure wants the skill
to tell them what a drawing fixes there, because the parts are sentences
and the only new thing may be an order; they will know when the skill says
that a text drawing fixes the section, the order and the words, and that
an order is a promise like any other.

## Assumptions

- Text changes are now most of what this league draws. Six of the last
  nine drawings were sentences in skill files, and the skill's guidance
  assumes something new exists to be structured.
- The skill already says that for a text change the parts are the
  sentences' places and the fixed words are what the contract reads. It
  says where; it does not say that an order is fixed too, and it does not
  say what a change joining an existing section owes to what is already
  there.
- An order is testable cheaply, by where each phrase first appears inside
  the section, which is two comparisons. The rule costs nothing to hold
  and it was the whole strength of one drawing this week.
- A change that joins an existing section can displace a sentence a closed
  task's contract reads. Naming what must not be disturbed is what stops
  that, and it was done by hand twice.
- These criteria are text in `skills/architect/SKILL.md`.

## Constraints

- The skill stays under its line budget and the standard's shape; no
  vendor, no dash (rules).
- No fixture of the architect skill carries a runner, so nothing is
  regenerated by this change.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The architect skill says that when the change is text, the drawing
   fixes three things: the section each sentence lives in, its order among
   the sentences already there, and the words the contract reads.
2. The skill says an order is a promise like any other and names how it is
   held: by where each phrase first appears in the section; and it says a
   change joining an existing section names what must not be disturbed
   there.
3. These five retros are under `retros/archive/` and none remains in
   `retros/`: `2026-09-06-architect-twenty-first-use.md`,
   `2026-09-06-architect-twenty-second-use.md`,
   `2026-09-06-architect-twenty-third-use.md`,
   `2026-09-06-architect-twenty-fifth-use.md`,
   `2026-09-07-architect-twenty-eighth-use.md`.

## Open questions

- How much may a section carry before a reader skims it rather than reads
  it (twenty-eighth)? A reopen condition was written at about thirty lines
  and the number was invented.
- Can two drawings from one run share a seam, so a promise they both hold
  is written once and tested once (thirtieth)?
- Should the league be able to say "this must be read before that" as
  something a wall holds, rather than a contract test someone chose to
  write (twenty-eighth)?
