# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the seventy second use of the analyse skill, amending criterion 4 of
`an-install-carries-the-method` so the step brings the league rather than one
member of it, 10 September 2026.
Place: this repository

## Liked

- The word chose the behaviour, and noticing that was the whole of it. Kai
  offered `assemble` against `summon`, and the two words point at two shapes:
  you summon one member and a group assembles. Picking the name picked
  whether the command places one skill or all of them, and the naming
  question turned out to be the design question wearing a costume.
- The criterion moved and no pin went `review-needed`, because the only
  drawing that reads it is still in an open pull request. First time this
  week a criterion has moved without costing anything, and it is cheap
  because the reading has not happened yet rather than because the rule
  softened.

## Learned

- A criterion's letter can contradict its own goal, and nothing in this tree
  reads one against the other. This page's goal says a consumer wants the
  league and not half of it, and its first assumption says a sub-package is
  an option to take less and never an obligation to assemble more. Criterion
  4 then said "a skill", singular, and the architect followed the letter into
  a command that hands a consumer six invocations, which is that obligation
  with a command in front of it.
- So the architect was right to follow the criterion and wrong to stop there.
  The Goal is not decoration; it is the thing the criteria are supposed to be
  a checkable reading of, and a drawing that satisfies a criterion while
  defeating the goal has found a defect in the criterion rather than a design.
- Fourth instance today of a proof asserting more than its criterion says,
  after `a-trace-pins-what-it-read` twice and `a-pin-says-who-cleared-it`
  once. This one is the other direction: the criterion itself over-specified
  and the test faithfully carried it. Four in one day is not four accidents.

## Lacked

- Nothing reads a criterion against the Goal above it. Every wall in this
  tree reads criteria against tests, tests against seams, and seams against
  code, and the top of the chain is prose nobody compares anything to.
- No name for a criterion that is narrower than its own goal. It is not
  vacuous, it is not stale, and it passes every wall; the only thing that
  catches it is a person reading both in one sitting.

## Longed for

- The manage skill, which is now decided and inside this release. The
  question that would have caught this one is small and sits in the analyst's
  own lane: does each criterion, read alone, still reach the Goal. Four of
  the last four defects were a seat not asking itself something before
  acting, and the retros have been saying so for twelve records.
