# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the eleventh use of the code skill, on `requirements/evals-v2`
(the evals workflow's endpoint as configuration, after the hosted service
was retired), 5 September 2026.

## Liked

- The diff in the workflow is the destination and the key; the body of the
  request and the record template are untouched, as the drawing fixed.
- The refusal is three lines of shell and says the two names.

## Learned

- A closed requirement can be superseded without being rewritten: its
  assumption and its criterion carry a parenthesis naming the newer task,
  and its test asserts the new truth. History stays readable.

## Lacked

  neither variable nor secret yet, so the build is argued by its text and
  the walls, not observed against an endpoint.
