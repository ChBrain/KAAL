# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the tenth use of the test skill, re-recording a suite a criterion
moved, 12 September 2026.
Place: this repository

## Liked

- One line changed, the sha, and the promotion went back to refusing nothing.
  The record's date was already today, so the only thing stale about it was
  the thing the record exists to pin.
- The architect's diff said this was owed and named it, rather than leaving it
  for whoever ran the promotion next. A block named by the seat that cannot
  clear it is the cheapest kind of handover there is.

## Learned

- One criterion moving costs four diffs across four seats. The analyst amends,
  the architect reads the region it pinned, the tester re-records the suite
  the amendment moved, and only then does the task read `delivered` again.
  None of the four is wrong and none can be skipped, and the whole of it was
  a criterion that stopped saying which directory a test lives in.
- A record can be same-day and stale. `runs` said `the record is stale: its
suite has changed since, and recorded today`, which is the report being
  exactly right and reading like a contradiction. The date says when somebody
  ran something; the sha says whether they ran this.

## Lacked

- One answer to what a task still owes. A pin awaiting a read and a stale
  record are two commands, and the promotion is the only thing that reads
  both. This is the second retro today to want it.

## Longed for

- The record to move with the suite it pins, where the suite moved inside one
  chain of diffs that nobody disputes. Four seats touched this task and every
  one of them knew the suite had changed.
