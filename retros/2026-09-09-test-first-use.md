# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the first use of the test skill in its own right, recording the
league's runs and answering for what the recording found, 9 September 2026.
Place: this repository

## Liked

- The command did its half in one line: 59 suites run, 59 records written,
  and the two tasks with no record are exactly the two nobody has built. The
  tree says something true about itself now that no page declares.
- Every claim that had to move kept its meaning. Eight seams and criteria
  changed what they read and not one of them changed what it promised, which
  is the difference between superseding a claim and abandoning it.

## Learned

- The board was green in the one state where it could not fail. With no
  record anywhere, no task can regress, so every red suite reads not
  delivered and the wall answers. The build that introduced records declared
  twelve walls green while holding zero of them, and three broken criteria
  went to main wearing the words "work in progress". This is the defect this
  league exists to catch, built by me and then walked into by me.
- Recording is not bookkeeping, it is the first time the wall has teeth. Nine
  more defects surfaced the moment evidence existed: three the moment I ran
  the command, five the moment the contracts wall had records to compare
  against, and one the recording caused. None of them was visible before.
- A record is evidence and not an artefact. Writing 53 of them into the
  tester's directory made the trace wall ask each one what it was made from,
  which is a question about provenance asked of a measurement. The place
  reads the strategy and the plans and skips the runs now.
- Splitting an act across lanes can split a proof from the thing it proves. I
  argued that recording belongs to the tester and the build belongs to the
  coder, and that is right about who does what and wrong about when a thing
  is proved. The build's own claim of green was true only because the tester
  had not yet arrived. A lane is a boundary of authority; it is not licence
  to call a wall green before it has anything to read.

## Lacked

- No word for a wall that cannot fail yet. Twelve walls green and one of them
  holding no evidence at all is not the same board as twelve walls green over
  a full tree, and nothing in the output tells them apart.
- Nothing about the order of a build and its first recording. The league says
  every seat writes its own proof; it does not say that a proof with nothing
  to read is not yet a proof.

## Longed for

- The board to say how much evidence each wall read. A count beside the
  verdict would have said in one line what took nine defects to learn.
- A first recording as part of a build's own handoff, or a rule that a task
  introducing evidence is not delivered until evidence exists. Either would
  have caught this before it reached main.
