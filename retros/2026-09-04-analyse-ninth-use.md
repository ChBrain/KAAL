# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the ninth use of the analyse skill, on `requirements/waiver-v1` (the
human gates as data, and waivers as recorded acts), 4 September 2026.

## Liked

- The waiver is a file in the tree with four fields; the board says waived,
  not ok; the history keeps who and why. That is auditable with nothing but
  git.
- An unused waiver is reported and not failed, and a waiver for a green wall
  is not a bug.

## Learned

- "May a human let a red wall through" had no answer in the design, and the
  answer that fits the league is yes, visibly, for a stated time, never
  silently; the runner's summary now has three counts, not two.

## Lacked

- A name for who may sign; v1 says anyone with push and the file names
  them.

## Longed for

- The three human gates being read by the seats that hold them, so
  operate's key and the architect's approval point at the same config.
