# Retrospective: 4 L's

Period: one-hundred-fourth use of analyse, restoring the durable-procedure
boundary in pull request 312 after review, 14 September 2026
Place: this repository

## Liked

- The review preserved the detailed control and authority work while naming
  the exact boundary that needed correction.
- The existing thirteen-criterion shape could express the correction without
  inventing another requirement or weakening prior proof.
- Explicit pre-dispatch and post-publish fixtures made the visibility timing
  contradiction observable.

## Learned

- A procedure may define target kinds and their control applicability while
  only a release input can own exact targets and derive its relation set.
- Changing a release candidate stales observations without changing the
  procedure that explains how to make them.
- A future expected package state is neither compliant nor noncompliant
  evidence before the package exists; it is not yet observable.
- Isolated semantic corruptions remained necessary after the tests changed,
  because the missing command makes every current-tree case share one red
  precondition.

## Lacked

- The earlier fixture put procedure rules and one release's identities in one
  document, which made their ownership look interchangeable.
- The full bug-aware board remains too slow to use as the first feedback on a
  focused Analyst correction.

## Longed for

- Separate normalized procedure and release-input schemas whose boundary is
  structural rather than carried by convention.
- A focused trace check that validates one changed acceptance case and its two
  dependent plans before the full board.
