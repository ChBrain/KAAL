# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the seventy-eighth use of the analyse skill, amending
`a-suite-names-its-cases` twice more: once because a comma list cannot carry
sixty five paths, and once because saying so was below this seat's layer,
11 September 2026.
Place: this repository

## Liked

- The measurement is what settled it rather than an opinion. Seeding the
  acceptance suite from its own glob is 3,557 characters on one line and 7,782
  once the shas are on, and the parser's sub key pattern takes a slash and not
  a dot, so a map is not available either. Both are runs anyone can repeat.
- The tree had already solved it once and said so. The `reviews:` block sits
  beside `traces:`, one entry to a line, and getting it there took widening
  that same pattern by one character class.

## Learned

- The asker was right that a comma list is architecture. The criteria said how
  a trace is written, which is a decision, and the skill's own rule is that a
  criterion naming a pattern has looked below its layer. What this task asks
  is that a plan traces its suites and a suite traces its cases, each carrying
  a sha; whether that is a line or a block belongs to the drawing.
- The sha was another task's criterion wearing this one's clothes. It answers
  knowing precisely what you refer to and knowing when it changed, which is
  `a-trace-pins-what-it-read`, closed and true of every trace. Stating it here
  would have been this task claiming a closed task's ground, and the test that
  asserted it was the seventh instance this tree has of a proof reaching past
  its own criterion.
- A criterion can be lifted above a decision and its proof cannot, quite. The
  acceptance tests still write a fixture in some shape, so they are written in
  the shape the drawing fixes and move with it. That is now an assumption on
  the page rather than a surprise for whoever changes the shape.
- Three stand-ins for one task, and each one found the next problem. The
  first found a test that could never go green; the second proved the field
  shape; the third is the only reason anybody measured the line before
  committing a document nobody could read.

## Lacked

- The line between the seats is still blurry and this task found three
  different places to put it in one afternoon: the writing of a trace, the sha
  on it, and the shape of a fixture. Each was moved by the asker rather than
  by a rule, and the asker has named it as work for the release after this
  one.
- No rule for where the line is between a criterion and a decision when the
  artefact is written by a person. A command's output line is fixed by the
  criterion because a caller parses it; a page's frontmatter is read by a
  person and by a tool, and the skill says nothing about which of those makes
  it surface.
- Nothing asks what a criterion costs at the size the tree will actually
  reach. Sixty five cases is the first number anyone computed here, and it
  arrived two amendments in.

## Longed for

- A step between writing a criterion and writing its test that runs the
  criterion against the tree's real counts. Two of today's three amendments
  would have been one.
