# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the seventy-sixth use of the architect skill, drawing what makes a
red case a regression, 13 September 2026.
Place: this repository

## Liked

- Three seams and every one of them a reader this tree already had. The join
  exists, the record reader exists, the freshness test exists; the whole
  drawing is about which of them the wall asks and in what order.
- The stand-in ran the superseded task's proof as well as the requirement's.
  Ten cases green across three layers before a line of the build, which is
  the habit the last drawing's reversal bought.

## Learned

- A module cannot borrow a reader from a module that borrows from it.
  `acceptance.mjs` imports `runs.mjs`, so the join living in the first cannot
  be imported by the second, and the cycle does not fail to load: it answers
  undefined and the reader returns false for every case. That reads exactly
  like a record that does not speak, which is the answer the wall is supposed
  to give sometimes, so the failure wore the shape of the feature.
- What the runs said was right and the conclusion from it was wrong. The
  drawing recorded that the join is built and exported and that both judged
  walls use it, and concluded the third could too. Every word of the fact was
  true and the inference needed one more thing nobody had written down.
- Agreement by shared input is not agreement by identity, and the record has
  to say which it bought. Reading the same two functions the verdict table
  reads means the two cannot disagree today and can drift the day the table
  gains a term. Naming that in the decision is the difference between a
  choice and a coincidence.

## Lacked

- Anything that reads the import graph. A cycle here is a silent false and
  the tree has no wall that would say so.

## Longed for

- A drawing that can cite a fact and its limits together. What the runs said
  is a list of things anyone can repeat, and the one that mattered was a
  thing about that fact rather than the fact itself.
