# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the fifth use of the test skill, reading the three cases that moved
under the three suites and recording the run that makes
`a-suite-names-its-cases` delivered, 11 September 2026.
Place: this repository

## Liked

- Three reads and every one was a true question. A case moved four times today
  and the suite that names it was asked, each time, whether it still covers
  the same case. It does, and now the page says who checked.
- The record lands with seven passing and nothing owed, which is the first
  time this task has been able to say that.

## Learned

- The ripple cost three extra reads and it is now a repeat rather than a
  curiosity. A plan pins its suite's whole file, and a suite's whole file
  holds its own reviews block, so recording three reads obliged three more of
  suites whose content no plan depends on. One case moving costs two lines
  every time, and one of the two is ceremony. The fix is general and is not
  in this release's plan: a `reviews:` block is a record about pins and should
  never be part of the region a pin above it reads.
- `runs --write` offered sixty three records where two were owed, for the
  third time this week, and the hand sorting that makes it safe is still in
  nobody's page.
- The last record could not be written and the tool was right to refuse it.
  `the-test-tree-is-written-down` is red because its own `paired` fixture
  still carries globs in its plan pages, and the new finding fires on them. A
  record written there would have read `regressed` and taken the wall with it.

## Lacked

- No word for a fixture left behind by a rule the tree just gained. The
  fixture is correct about the tree it was built for and wrong about the tree
  it now runs in, and nothing says whose job that is until a suite goes red.

## Longed for

- A pin that reads what a page claims rather than what a page contains, so
  that recording a read is not itself a change worth reading.
