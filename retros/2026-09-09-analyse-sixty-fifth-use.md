# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the sixty-fifth use of the analyse skill, on
`requirements/a-seat-claims-what-it-covers` (seven criteria, the four rows
the ask named), 9 September 2026.
Place: this repository

## Liked

- Every number the ask asks for was already computable and none was reported.
  Counting them took one shell loop and the requirement could then argue
  from facts rather than about whether a gap exists: 88 per cent of
  requirements answered by a drawing, 96 per cent with a run on record.
- The gaps turned out to be specific and unfindable by reading. Seven
  requirements have no drawing at all, five of them closed work that shipped
  without one, and the seventh is mine from four hours ago. A percentage
  would have hidden that; the names are the report.

## Learned

- Writing the requirement made me the first person caught by it. I built
  `the-publish-carries-a-token` without a drawing because it was two lines of
  workflow, and it is in the undrawn list beside five older ones. The
  measurement does not care that my reason was good.
- A number with no names is a report that tells a reader they have a problem
  and not where it is. Criterion 3 exists because the first draft of this
  requirement asked only for the percentage, and a percentage is the half of
  the answer nobody can act on.
- The report must not fail, and saying why took longer than saying it. A
  coverage number is a fact about how far the work has got, not a promise
  anybody made, and there is no threshold in the ask. What could fail is a
  claim about the number, and the open question notes that a coverage record
  pinned like a run would make a drop a regression rather than a policy.
- Criterion 4 is green before the build and it is a guard, not a defect. No
  page declares a coverage number today; the test exists so that adding one
  later is a red rather than a habit. Naming that in the handoff is the
  difference between a guard and a criterion pointed at nothing.

## Lacked

- Nothing in the skill about a criterion whose fixture is the league itself.
  Criterion 7 counts this tree and compares it against the command's rows,
  and that is the only place the real numbers can be checked. It is also the
  criterion most likely to need editing every time the tree grows.
- No shape for a row. Each seat's row carries a name, a subject, a count and
  a share, and the criteria describe that in prose four times over rather
  than once.

## Longed for

- The undrawn list to be a finding somewhere. Five closed tasks shipped
  without a drawing and nothing in this league has ever said so; this task
  reports it and does not refuse it, which is right for a first report and
  will not stay right.
- A count of what a wall read, beside what it found. The tester's retro asked
  for it this afternoon and this task is the shape of the answer.
