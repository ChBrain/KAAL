# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the sixty-second use of the architect skill, amending one contract of
`an-artefact-traces-what-it-came-from` so the kind table can grow from
another lane without a red wall in between, 11 September 2026.
Place: this repository

## Liked

- The guard was kept rather than dropped. The contract pinned the table's rows
  by name on purpose, so that a kind added by accident is still a red, and the
  fix keeps that: every row must be one the league agreed to, and the four
  this drawing fixed must still be there. An equality became two containments
  and nothing was softened.
- The comment that explained the choice is what made the amendment safe. It
  said "named rather than counted, so a kind added by accident is still a
  red", and without it the obvious move was to delete the assertion.

## Learned

- A contract that pins a set by equality demands its whole content from one
  diff. The kinds arrive in the developer's lane and the contract lives in
  the architect's, so an equality here is a promise that two lanes land
  together, which the seats wall forbids. Two containments say the same thing
  and survive the gap.
- The gap is the thing to design for and it has now appeared three times in
  one task. Standing a check down before its replacement, moving a case out of
  one seat's tree into another's, and now growing a table a contract counts:
  each is one change that cannot be one diff, and each wants a middle state
  that is honest rather than merely green.

## Lacked

- No word for a promise the league has agreed to and not yet written. The
  contract now names six kinds where four exist, which is exactly right and
  reads, to anyone who has not seen the requirement, like a list with two
  mistakes in it.

## Longed for

- A contract that could name the lane a row is coming from. `suites` and
  `cases` are in a merged requirement and a merged drawing, and the contract
  can only say their names.

Tensions weighed: `the-two-goods`, once. Keeping the guard costs a reader the
plain equality and buys the tree the ability to grow a table across two lanes.
No tension went unnamed.
