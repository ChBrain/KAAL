# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the seventy-first use of the architect skill, reading the criterion
this drawing was already written for, 11 September 2026.
Place: this repository

## Liked

- One line, and it is the last thing standing between this task and a verdict
  a promotion would accept. An unread pin reads `not delivered`, and
  `not delivered` is precisely what the gate this task defines refuses.

## Learned

- The read is the second half of an order nobody chose. Seam 9 had to be drawn
  before criterion 9 could merge, so the drawing pinned a criteria region that
  did not yet hold the criterion, and the pin went stale the moment it did.
  That is not a criterion moving underneath a seam; it is the only permitted
  order catching up with itself, and the review says so rather than claiming
  nothing happened.
- Nothing would have cleared this on its own. The rest of this task is the
  developer's, so no architect diff was coming, and the pin would have sat
  through the build and been found at the promotion by the wall it was written
  to feed.

## Lacked

- A reason for this diff to exist other than bookkeeping. It carries one line
  of frontmatter and a retro about it.

## Longed for

- A pin that can be written ahead of the region it names, for the case where
  the order of two lanes is fixed and known. The read was foreseeable the hour
  seam 9 was drawn.
