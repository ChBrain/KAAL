Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the fourth real use of the analyse skill, on the eval record's
contract (`requirements/eval-record-v1`), 4 September 2026.

## Liked

- The ask came from outside the league (a review) and still read as one
  task: what a record must carry to count. Four criteria, three fixture
  roots, no invented scope.
- Extending freshness from the skill to the fixture cost one sentence in the
  requirement and two fields in the record.

- A contract that is about to hold evidence must be written before the
  first evidence exists; that ordering was the whole reason to take this
  task ahead of the manual step.
- The record's required field list will live in two places, a document and
  the ledger check, and the constraint says so; a wall that the document
  and the code agree is the architect's to draw.
