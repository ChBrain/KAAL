# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the eleventh use of the analyse skill, on `requirements/evals-v2`
(the evals workflow's endpoint as configuration, after the hosted service
was retired), 5 September 2026.

## Liked

- The ask came as a workflow log; the requirement's first paragraph quotes
  the error and dates the retirement, so the goal is argued from a fact and
  not from a preference.
- A `Supersedes:` line in the Handoff, the thing the fourth retro lacked:
  push-v1's assumption and its criterion 8 are named as superseded and
  edited to say so, not silently contradicted.

## Learned

- A host named in the tree is a vendor named in the tree, and the vendor
  rule already said no; the retirement made the spelling visible. A URL is
  configuration.
- A provider's error ("brownout") described a service that no longer
  existed; the workflow's own refusal has to say what to configure, because
  nothing upstream will.

## Lacked

- A wall that reads a workflow's external hosts the way `reach` reads a
  script's imports; this defect was found by running, not by reading.
