# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the twenty-eighth use of the analyse skill, on
`requirements/asks-when-not-told` (one criterion, one checklist line), 6
September 2026.
Place: this repository

## Liked

- The ask was already agreed: it was item one of a list I put to Kai and he
  said go, so nothing had to be interpreted and the scope was settled
  before the first line.
- One criterion for a one-line change, and the skill did not push me to
  invent a second to look thorough. The count is honest.

## Learned

- The cheapest test of a behaviour we just shipped was a checklist that
  already existed and had gone blind to it. A skill change silently widens
  what every fixture should judge, and the fixture keeps passing while
  judging the old thing.
- Writing the open question about sweeping all fifteen checklists was
  better than doing it. The sweep needs a rule behind it or the sixteenth
  fixture arrives without the line, and a rule is a task, not a line.

## Lacked

- Nothing in the skill says how small a task may be before the three seat
  chain costs more reading than the change. I decided by proportion, and
  the reason ended up in the drawing, which is the architect's page and
  not mine.
- No way to see which fixtures a skill change made blind. Six skills moved
  yesterday; which of the fifteen checklists now judge less than they
  should is a memory exercise, and I found this one by remembering.

## Longed for

- A `kaal` reading that lists, for a skill that moved, the fixtures whose
  checklists mention nothing about what changed. Even a crude one would
  have found this without me.
