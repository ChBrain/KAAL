# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the twenty-seventh use of the analyse skill, on
`requirements/where-a-skill-acts` (the skill rung of the two places), 6
September 2026.

## Liked

- The ask arrived as four lines and needed no interpretation: two places,
  results local in the first, undecided in the second, and the retro in
  the league either way. The requirement quotes them and adds nothing.
- The undecided part stayed undecided. Kai said where results go in the
  second place needs discussing, so it is the first open question and no
  assumption pretends to settle it; the skills ask instead.

## Learned

- A criterion on a template must say whether the files already written to
  it change. The retro format gains a `Place:` line, and without an
  assumption naming the sixty retros already filed, a developer would
  reasonably sweep them all and call it the criterion.

## Lacked

- Nothing new.

## Longed for

- A record for a use of a skill on a directory it was pointed at, the way
  an eval run has one. The retro carries the lesson, but nothing carries
  the run.
