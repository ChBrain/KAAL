# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the sixty-fifth use of the architect skill, reading the criteria that
moved under `the-test-tree-is-written-down`'s drawing and recording what it
cost, 11 September 2026. One line of work and one thing worth keeping.
Place: this repository

## Liked

- The read was quick because the drawing had already answered. The counts seam
  was withdrawn hours before the criteria it served were amended, so the
  question was only whether anything left in the picture reads either clause,
  and nothing does.

## Learned

- The pin arrived after the work rather than before it. The supersede was
  agreed, the seam withdrawn, the criterion amended, and only then did the pin
  ask whether the drawing had noticed. That is the right order for a league
  where one person moves between seats and the wrong one for a league where
  the architect learns of the change from the pin: read forwards it is a
  reminder, read backwards it is the only notice anybody gets.

## Lacked

- No way to record a review before the region moves. The architect knew this
  supersede was coming and had already acted on it, and still had to wait for
  the pin to go red to say so.

## Longed for

- A review a seat can write in advance of the move it expects, cleared by the
  move rather than by a second visit.

Tensions weighed: none. Nothing was chosen here; the reading was already done
and this records it.
