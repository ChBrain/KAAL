# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the eighty-seventh use of the analyse skill, stating
`a-plan-picks-its-suites`, 12 September 2026.
Place: this repository

## Liked

- One criterion of six was written wrong and caught before anything was built.
  It said a promotion refuses only what the regression plan reaches, which
  would have narrowed a gate a closed task already fixed, and rereading
  `a-promotion-names-what-it-refuses` is what found it rather than a wall.
- The tension the whole task turns on came out as a red test rather than as
  an argument. A plan about a wall whose command names no `.test.mjs` file
  answers `is about the wall regression, which no gate in kaal.config.json
holds`, and that is the open question printing itself.

## Learned

- A criterion can contradict a closed one and no wall reads for it. What I
  wrote would have excused a red case outside the selection from refusing a
  promotion, and criterion 5 of the promotion task says no wall may stand red
  there at all. Two pages, one contradiction, and the only thing that catches
  it is somebody opening both.
- The delta is about ownership and I nearly wrote it as narrowing. What the
  plan item says is that the seat owning the method does not own the
  selection, which is a question of who declares it. Reading it as "protect
  less" is the easy misreading and it is the one that breaks something.
- The fifth case passed on the wrong rule at first. A path under `evals/` was
  a finding because the fixture's config gave it no owner, so the case was
  green on ownership and silent about verification. A lane that owns `evals/`
  in the fixture made it red for its own reason.

## Lacked

- Any reading of the closed criteria a new one touches. The skill says to read
  the closed requirements whose paths this task touches, and this task touches
  a gate rather than a path, so nothing pointed at the page that contradicted
  it.

## Longed for

- A wall that reads two criteria against each other. The tree has walls for a
  criterion with no test, a case no suite names and a plan with no wall, and
  nothing for two criteria that cannot both be true.
