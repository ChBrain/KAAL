# Retrospective: 4 L's

Self-diagnosis: drawn from session context. Not a human-reported retro.

Period: the eighteenth use of the analyse skill, on `requirements/standard-v1` (compliance with the
Agent Skills standard, tested in both directions), 5 September 2026.

## Liked

- The ask was one sentence and the requirement could name both directions
  of divergence from it: the tree against the standard, and the standard
  against the pin. Each got its own seam and its own red.
- The standard's own validator ran over the six skills before a line was
  written, so the ask started from a fact: compliant today, untested.

## Learned

- A mirror of a standard is a claim with a date on it; the pin made the
  claim checkable and the command makes the check cheap. What was missing
  was never the rule, it was the thing that hits when the rule goes stale.

## Lacked

- Nothing new.

## Longed for

- The standard cutting releases, so the validator pins to a tag.
