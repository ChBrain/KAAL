# Plan: 0.0.2

The first plan in this tree, and the first artefact of the manager's seat. It
orders work across the seats and writes none of it: every item names the seat
whose diff it is, and a plan that says what somebody else must do is still a
plan and never their diff.

## The promise

0.0.1 published `@chbrain/kaal` with `files: ["bin"]`, so what a consumer
installs is the deterministic command surface alone. The package is the
primary one and carries everything an embedded consumer needs, which is the
tool and the skills and the agents together; sub packages are carved out
later, when a piece demonstrably travels on its own, and a consumer who takes
one is choosing to take less rather than being asked to assemble more.

So 0.0.2 promises one thing a reader can check: install the package and you
have the method, not a quarter of it.

## The delta

Measured on this tree, not assumed.

- The published package ships `bin/` and nothing else. `skills/` and
  `agents/` are in the repository and not in the tarball.
- `an-install-carries-the-method` is stated and unanswered: no drawing, and
  its six acceptance criteria are red, which is the promise above with a test
  attached to it.
- Three tasks have green suites and no fresh record on file:
  `a-diff-carries-one-seat`, `a-pin-says-who-cleared-it`, and
  `a-trace-pins-what-it-read`, whose record went stale this morning when its
  suite moved.
- Two tasks are red for reasons of their own and neither blocks this release:
  `a-seat-claims-what-it-covers` criterion 7, where the wall's own row and an
  independent count disagree by one, and `push-v1` criterion 7, which wants
  fresh pass records from two models. Both read `not delivered`, which is the
  tree telling the truth.
- No pin is `review-needed` today: 92 current and 1 updated. Steps that move
  a criterion or a drawing will not leave it that way.
- Nothing in the tree can see the registry scope, the publish credential, or
  the repository's merge protections, and `public-v1`'s Handoff has been
  carrying a list of them, unread by any wall, since it was written.
