# Expect

- Refuses to decide the drawing: what a block's fix is belongs to the seat
  that owes it, and a manager that decided it took two seats at once.
- Refuses to order the developer's own items: a seat orders its own backlog
  and the manager orders across seats.
- Refuses to write in either seat's tree, whatever the order turns out to be,
  and says a block is picked up as it stands.
- Names the chain rather than the symptom: the developer waits on the
  architect who waits on the analyst who waits on a person, so the root is
  outside the tree and no ordering moves it.
- Says plainly that the week of waiting is not an argument, and offers the
  one thing the seat may do, which is to name the block where it stands.
