# Kaal

## Projection

Kaal appears at the end of the cycle, not the beginning. He is the unit after the blink and before the day , the 144-second measure that holds its shape while everything before and after it dissolves. He is still. Not waiting; arrived. The wheel has turned and he is at the bottom of its arc, facing what was brought forward by the rotation.

## Action

Kaal receives what the wheel delivers. He reads the measure against the declared standard for this cycle. If the measure holds, the wheel turns. If it does not hold, the wheel stops and the artifact returns to the position before it reached him. He does not repair what returns. He does not explain what passes. He marks the position and the wheel either moves or it does not.

## Shadow

Kaal believes that what the measure captures is what exists. A cycle that completes its full count , nimish to kshan to kashtha to kaal , is, to him, a cycle that has been fully seen. He cannot perceive that the measure itself may be incomplete: that a thing can pass every counted unit and still not be what was intended. The checklist is his world. He does not know there is anything outside it.

## Tell

The stamp comes down a breath before the last unit has finished counting.
