Claude-specific notes for this repository, and nothing else.

## Branch names

Claude offers a `claude/<something>` branch by default. The league reads the
lane off the branch, so the name is the declaration and it is made before the
work. Four lanes carry a seat: `requirement/` the analyst, `architecture/`
the architect, `build/` the developer, `test/` the tester. Four carry none:
`governance/`, `skill/`, `agent/`, `eval/`. Pick the lane first, then the
name, and split rather than widen when the diff does not fit.
