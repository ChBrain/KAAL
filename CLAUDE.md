Claude-specific notes for this repository, and nothing else.

## Branch names

Claude offers a `claude/<something>` branch by default. The league names a
branch by the lane of its diff (`requirement/`, `governance/`, `skill/`,
`agent/`, `eval/`); pick the lane first, then the name.
